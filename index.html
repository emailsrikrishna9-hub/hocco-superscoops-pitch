The honest claim is an edge

The shiny land ofPackaging

H2Taste

"Tastes like Hocco" is the unmet need: buyers will not trade taste for protein.

Test

Pass bar
≥ 60% prefer Superscoops over rivals; ≥ 80% parity with HOCCO regular
If it fails

H3Price

Move the levers yourself.

Pick cities, SKUs, prices, channels and spend. The planner returns a 12-week P&L, a payback week, the gate checks and a go or no-go.

What moves the numbers
  • Cost to make +₹6 (protein or sweetener prices): the quick-commerce margin falls from 39% to 30%, and gate H6 triggers the fallback.
  • Price at ₹89: volume ~+10% (est.), but ₹6.7 less per cup, so contribution falls ~16%.
  • Sales 30% below plan: wave 1 contribution drops to ~₹1.7 Cr, and wave 2 waits.
  • Double the marketing to ₹4 L per city per week: the planner's call flips to NO-GO.
